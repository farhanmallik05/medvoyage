# MedVoyage — Marketplace Business Strategy & Commercial Model

> **EthAum Venture Partners Track**  
> **Target Alignment**: Equity, Paid Internships, and Revenue Sharing Opportunities

---

## 1. Value Proposition
- **For Patients**: Peace of mind, up to 70% surgical savings compared to US/UK private care, JCI-certified hospitals, transparent guaranteed package pricing, and structured aftercare.
- **For Accredited Hospitals**: High-margin international patient acquisition with pre-qualified medical records, reduced administrative concierge costs, and streamlined billing.

---

## 2. Monetization & Revenue Model
1. **Hospital Marketplace Success Commission (8% - 12%)**:
   - Hospital pays MedVoyage a performance-based platform fee upon confirmed patient admission.
   - Zero patient markups: patient pays the direct hospital-negotiated rate.
2. **Telehealth Consultation Gating Fee ($75 - $150)**:
   - Modest fee for direct video consultation with overseas chief surgeons (credited back to the surgical package upon booking).
3. **Value-Added Concierge Upgrades**:
   - Medical visa expedited processing, companion hotel bookings, airport tarmac ambulance transfers, and bilingual medical nursing attendants.

---

## 3. Provider Quality Verification Framework
1. **Accreditation Mandate**: Must possess Joint Commission International (JCI), Australian Healthcare Standards (ACHS), or national tertiary NABH/Temos accreditation.
2. **Surgeon Credentialing**: Independent verification of board certifications (e.g., FRCS, American Board, European Board), malpractice insurance standing, and minimum 500+ successful procedure tenure.
3. **Outcome Auditing**: Annual reviews of complication rates, surgical site infection indices, and verified patient satisfaction scores.
