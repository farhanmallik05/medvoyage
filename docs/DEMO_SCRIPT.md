# MedVoyage — Demonstration Video Script

> **EthAum Venture Partners | Medical Tourism Marketplace**  
> **Target Video Length**: 3 - 4 Minutes

---

## Storyboard & Script Breakdown

### Part 1: The Cross-Border Healthcare Dilemma (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual**: Headlines showing rising US surgical costs ($38,000 for knee replacement) and UK NHS wait times (18+ months).
- **Voiceover**: "Every year, millions of patients seek surgical care overseas. But today's medical travel websites are little more than lead-generation directories filled with vague 'from' pricing, hidden facility surcharges, and total abandonment once the patient is discharged. Patients are left wondering: What will this actually cost? Who is my surgeon? And what happens when I fly back home?"

### Part 2: Introducing MedVoyage (0:45 - 1:30)
- **Visual**: MedVoyage responsive homepage showing treatment categories and transparent comparison.
- **Voiceover**: "Meet MedVoyage. MedVoyage is the transparent medical tourism marketplace. Here, patients explore accredited international destinations—from Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi to Apollo Hospitals in India and Bumrungrad in Bangkok. Everything is upfront: no hidden fees, JCI-accredited centers only, and verified surgeon credentials."

### Part 3: The Differentiator — Side-by-Side Itemized Comparison (1:30 - 2:30)
- **Visual**: Drilling into the Total Knee Replacement comparison matrix.
- **Voiceover**: "Look at our side-by-side comparison for Total Knee Replacement. Instead of a single opaque number, MedVoyage decomposes the package into procedure fees, surgeon professional fees, private inpatient suites, and anesthesia. Even more critical: we explicitly disclose potential additional expenses—such as extra ICU nights or specialized medications. Patients know the exact reality before boarding a flight."

### Part 4: AI Matching, Telehealth Booking & 90-Day Aftercare (2:30 - 3:30)
- **Visual**: Running the AI Provider Matching Engine on an inquiry, booking a telehealth consultation, and viewing the 90-day recovery timeline.
- **Voiceover**: "Our AI provider matching engine balances clinical specialty, budget boundaries, and travel logistics to recommend the optimal surgeon. With one click, patients schedule a direct telehealth video consultation. And after the procedure, MedVoyage guides patients through a structured 90-day aftercare plan, ensuring remote surgical follow-up long after they return home."

### Part 5: Conclusion & EthAum Opportunity (3:30 - 4:00)
- **Visual**: Team slide with LinkedIn profiles and marketplace metrics.
- **Voiceover**: "MedVoyage transforms cross-border medical travel into a trusted, transparent, and continuous healthcare experience. We are thrilled to partner with EthAum Venture Partners to scale this platform globally. Thank you."
