1. Product Vision 
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
━━━━━━━━━━━ 
To build a trusted, end-to-end international healthcare marketplace that enables patients to confidently 
discover and evaluate healthcare providers across borders. The platform will simplify the healthcare journey 
by helping patients understand treatment options, compare providers based on clinical expertise, outcomes, 
and costs, securely share medical records, obtain transparent treatment quotes, and communicate directly 
with qualified doctors and healthcare professionals. 
 
PATIENT-CENTRIC OUTCOME​
The platform aims to bring greater transparency, accessibility, and efficiency to international healthcare by 
connecting patients with suitable providers through a secure and patient-centric digital experience. From initial 
provider discovery and medical evaluation to treatment coordination and post-treatment follow-up, the product 
will create a connected ecosystem that supports informed decision-making and continuity of care.

1. Discover​
The patient visits the platform to search for a specific treatment, medical condition, doctor, hospital, or destination. They can 
explore available providers, treatments, estimated costs, reviews, accreditations, and locations. 
 
2. Create Case​
The patient creates a personal medical case by providing basic information such as their treatment requirement, diagnosis, 
preferred destination, budget, medical history, and expected travel dates. 
 
3. Upload Records​
The patient securely uploads relevant medical documents such as diagnostic reports, prescriptions, scans, previous 
treatment records, and other information required by healthcare providers. 
 
4. Qualification​
The platform reviews the patient's requirements and information to understand the type of treatment needed, urgency, 
budget, preferred location, and other relevant preferences. Additional information may be requested if required. 
 
5. Provider Matching​
Based on the patient's case, the platform identifies suitable hospitals, clinics, and doctors. Providers can be matched based 
on specialization, credentials, accreditation, treatment availability, location, pricing, and international-patient experience. 
 
6. Quote Comparison​
The patient receives treatment proposals or quotes from suitable providers. The platform allows them to compare treatment 
costs, inclusions, doctor details, estimated duration, accommodation, tests, medications, and other relevant expenses. 
 
7. Shortlist 2–3 Hospitals 
The patient shortlists 2–3 hospitals based on the initial information and quotations. No final provider decision is made yet. 
 
8. Exploratory Virtual Calls​
 The patient has exploratory virtual calls with the shortlisted hospitals.

9. Final Provider Selection 
After the exploratory calls, the patient evaluates the shortlisted providers and finalizes one hospital/provider.  
 
10. Booking & Upfront Payment 
The patient books the selected provider by paying an upfront booking/slot fee. This payment secures the patient's 
treatment/consultation slot with the selected hospital.  
 
11. Formal Virtual Medical Consultation 
After the booking fee is paid, the patient proceeds with the proper/official virtual consultation with the selected doctor or 
medical team.  
 
12.Treatment 
Following the formal consultation and subsequent confirmation, the patient proceeds with treatment at the selected hospital.  
 
13. Aftercare 
EthAum facilitates follow-up consultations, recovery information, medical documentation and continued communication with 
the healthcare provider.  
 
14. Review & Referral​
The platform will include a referral program through which clinics, hospitals, nurses, doctors, and other 
approved healthcare partners can refer patients to the platform for medical treatments. When a referred 
patient successfully completes the booking and payment process, the referring partner will be eligible to 
receive a 5% referral commission on the billed amount, subject to the platform’s referral terms and 
eligibility criteria. The system will track the referral source, patient booking, payment status, and 
commission automatically, allowing referral partners to monitor their eligible referrals and payouts. This 
model is designed to create a structured partner network that encourages healthcare professionals and 
providers to bring qualified patients to the platform.

CRM ANALYTICS​
CRM Analytics is a dashboard that helps the medical tourism platform track and analyze the complete patient and 
provider journey from the first enquiry to completed treatment. It provides insights into lead volume and sources, 
provider response time, quote conversion, booking conversion, revenue generated, cancellations, patient 
satisfaction, and provider service performance. The system can also track which marketing channels generate the 
most qualified leads, identify where patients drop off in the booking journey, and compare provider performance. 
These insights help the management team improve lead allocation, optimize sales and follow-up processes, identify 
high-performing providers, and make data-driven decisions to increase bookings and revenue
